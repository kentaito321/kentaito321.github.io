I am a PhD candidate at Keio University, Japan, under the supervision of Prof. Hideo Saito. I received the M.S. degree in information and computer science from Keio University, and the Engineering degree (Diplôme d’Ingénieur) in industrial engineering from the École Centrale de Nantes, Nantes, France, in 2025. I am interested in topics around 3D reconstruction, camera pose estimation, and representation learning.
